Icon packs: Glimpse Colour (Glimpse's own colourful icons) is built in, and Nova-style icon packs work too. Change any single icon from its menu, even to a picture; calendar icons show the date. Folders can show a small badge (music, games, work and more), in mono or colour. Menu text size: make the text in Settings and menus smaller or larger; buttons and menus fit narrow screens.
