Glimpse Launcher Plus is a fast, tidy home screen you can make your own.

Choose big tiles or a dense grid, icons on cards or straight on your wallpaper, or a calm screen of names. Keep it all safe behind a padlock, with a PIN if you want one.

WHAT YOU GET

• Your grid. 2 × 4 big tiles or 4 × 4 compact ones, or tiles off for 4 × 5, 5 × 5 and 5 × 6; your apps, folders and widgets keep their order.
• Your look. Tiles behind the icons on or off, a round, square or rounded square behind each icon, and name and icon sizes on simple sliders. Sizes fit each grid by themselves.
• Icon packs. Glimpse Colour, Glimpse's own colourful icons, is built in, and Nova-style icon packs work too. Change any single icon, even to a picture of your own; calendar icons show the date.
• Big icons. Make any app, folder, All apps, Search or Emergency 2 × 2; a big folder shows its apps, ready to open.
• Text mode. Swap the tiles for one calm screen of app names, with its own widgets screen; your tile screens wait unchanged. The Text mode switch widget swaps with one touch.
• Notes, one swipe away. Coloured cards with tick-box lines and pictures, to share, fold or keep behind your PIN; kept only on your phone.
• Quick gestures. Swipe down for your notifications, swipe up for All apps.
• Hold actions. Hold a tile (while locked) to switch the torch, open a web page, call or text, open another app, or jump to a settings page.
• A padlock that means it. Hold it to unlock and rearrange; tap to lock. Add a 4 to 8 digit PIN if you like: wrong tries pause the pad, and a forgotten PIN is reset with the phone's own screen lock.
• All apps, organised. Search as you type, tags with a filter, and, when unlocked, App info and Uninstall a hold away.
• Search. Find apps, settings and help as you type, file names in folders you choose, and your own web searches.
• Folders. Group apps, put them in any order, and give a folder a small badge (music, games, work and more).
• Widgets and free layout. Live widgets in sizes that fit, and icons in any square you choose.
• Your colours. Eight ready-made themes with matching wallpapers, or your own colours and transparency for tiles, icon squares, names, the bar, the clock and the highlight. Save your own themes, give one tile its own colours, place any wallpaper picture yourself, and give each screen its own.
• Clear Settings. Five groups, each setting on its own page with a short summary of what is set, a search box that finds any setting by name, and menu text you can make smaller or larger.
• Clock, numbers and full screen. Three clock styles and sizes, waiting-notification counts on icons (optional), and a full-screen mode.
• Backup and restore. Save your whole setup, notes and themes to one file, and put it back on this phone or a new one.
• An Emergency button (optional). A countdown you can cancel, then one call to the number you chose.
• Help when you need it. Report a bug or suggest a feature by email, with an optional bug log kept on your phone.

WHAT PLUS ADDS

• All files and folders. Search finds any file or folder in the phone's own storage by its name, as you type, and opens it (memory cards, hidden files, Android/data and Android/obb left out). Off until you switch it on.
• Two extra permissions, each for one thing. All files access, which you allow yourself, is used only to read file and folder names for Search: never what is in a file, and nothing is changed or deleted. Install unknown apps lets an .apk found by Search be opened; Android asks before every install.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

No advertisements, no accounts and no internet use. Everything you set up stays on the phone. A PIN is kept only as a scrambled hash.

HOW TO START

Install Glimpse Launcher Plus, follow the five short welcome pages, and choose it as your home screen when the phone asks. You can go back to your old home screen at any time in the phone's Settings.
